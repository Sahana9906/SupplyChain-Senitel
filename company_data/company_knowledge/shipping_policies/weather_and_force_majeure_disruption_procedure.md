# Weather and Force Majeure Disruption Procedure

Where a carrier or supplier reports a weather event, port closure, or
other force majeure condition affecting an active shipment, Logistics
should:

1. Request a revised estimated arrival date directly from the carrier.
2. Update the shipment record with the revised date and a note
   referencing the reported cause, in the carrier's own words.
3. Notify Warehouse Planning if the affected SKU is within 10 days of
   its safety stock threshold at the destination warehouse.

Force majeure delays are excluded from standard carrier on-time
performance scoring, subject to documentation from the carrier.
