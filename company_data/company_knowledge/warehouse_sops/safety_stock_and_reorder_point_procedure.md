# Safety Stock and Reorder Point Procedure

Each SKU carried at a given facility has a defined safety stock and
reorder point in the inventory system, calculated from historical daily
demand and the relevant supplier's agreed lead time.

When on-hand inventory falls below the reorder point, the system
generates a replenishment request to the assigned Buyer. The Buyer must
review and either issue a purchase order or document why replenishment
is not required within 1 business day.
