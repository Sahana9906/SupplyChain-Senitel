# Receiving Standard Operating Procedure

Applies to all facilities (WH-01, WH-02, WH-03, WH-04, WH-05, WH-06, WH-07, WH-08, WH-09, WH-10).

1. Verify inbound shipment against the associated purchase order before
   unloading; confirm carrier, PO reference, and expected quantity match
   the shipment record.
2. Log received quantities against the purchase order item record.
   Discrepancies exceeding 2% of expected quantity require a Receiving
   Exception report before stock is released.
3. Move received stock to the designated staging area within 4 hours of
   physical receipt.
4. Update on-hand inventory in the warehouse management system the same
   business day as receipt.
