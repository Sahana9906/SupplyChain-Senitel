# Logistics Agreement — Northern Star Freight

**Carrier:** Northern Star Freight (CAR-12)
**Service Type:** Parcel
**Coverage Region:** North America

## Service Terms

Northern Star Freight provides Parcel freight services under
this agreement. Tracked on-time performance for this carrier is
approximately 96%, with average transit time of
17.2 days for standard lanes in scope.

## Rate Structure

Rates are negotiated quarterly based on volume commitments. Fuel
surcharges apply per standard industry index and are reviewed monthly.

## Service-Level Commitments

The carrier commits to providing proactive status notification for any
shipment expected to miss its promised delivery date by more than 2
business days. Carrier performance is reviewed quarterly against
on-time delivery and damage-claim rate targets.
