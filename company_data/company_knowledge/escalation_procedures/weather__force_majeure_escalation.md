# Weather / Force Majeure Escalation

1. Logistics Coordinator logs any carrier- or supplier-reported weather
   or force majeure event affecting an active shipment, referencing the
   affected shipment ID(s).
2. Coordinator requests a revised estimated arrival date and updates the
   shipment record accordingly.
3. If the affected SKU is within 10 days of its safety stock threshold
   at the destination warehouse, notify Warehouse Planning per the
   Weather and Force Majeure Disruption Procedure.
