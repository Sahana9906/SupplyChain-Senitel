# Delayed Shipment Escalation

1. Logistics Coordinator identifies a shipment exceeding its promised
   delivery date by more than 3 business days.
2. Coordinator contacts the carrier for status within 1 business day.
3. If unresolved after 2 additional business days, escalate to the
   Logistics Manager.
4. Logistics Manager contacts the supplier account team directly and
   notifies the customer-facing team if downstream customer orders may
   be affected.
