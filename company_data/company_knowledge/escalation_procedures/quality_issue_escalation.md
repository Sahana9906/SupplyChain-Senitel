# Quality Issue Escalation

1. Warehouse receiving staff flag any lot failing incoming inspection as
   a quality hold.
2. Quality team opens a supplier quality notice and notifies the
   assigned Buyer.
3. Buyer holds further purchase orders to that supplier for the affected
   SKU pending the supplier's disposition response.
4. Two or more quality notices against the same supplier within a
   rolling 90-day period escalate to the Procurement Director for
   supplier review.
