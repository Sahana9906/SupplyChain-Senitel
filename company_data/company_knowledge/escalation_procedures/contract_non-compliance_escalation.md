# Contract Non-Compliance Escalation

1. Any confirmed deviation from a supplier's contractual SLA (delivery,
   quality, or quantity terms) is logged against the relevant contract
   record.
2. Category Manager reviews logged deviations at each quarterly business
   review with the supplier.
3. Repeated or material non-compliance is escalated to the Procurement
   Director, who determines whether penalty terms under the contract
   apply and whether contract renegotiation is warranted.
