# Customer Priority Order Escalation

1. Priority and VIP customer orders are flagged automatically in the
   order management system at time of order.
2. Warehouse allocation staff review flagged orders daily to confirm
   inventory availability against promised delivery date.
3. Where inventory is insufficient, the Warehouse Manager coordinates
   with Customer Service on delivery expectations and may request
   expedited replenishment from Procurement per the Expedited Shipping
   Policy.
