# Stockout Risk Escalation

1. Inventory falling below reorder point triggers an automated alert to
   the assigned Buyer.
2. Buyer reviews open purchase orders for that SKU and confirms expected
   arrival date against projected stockout date.
3. If no purchase order is open, or the expected arrival is beyond the
   projected stockout date, the Buyer escalates to the Category Manager
   for Emergency Purchase Order authorization per policy.
