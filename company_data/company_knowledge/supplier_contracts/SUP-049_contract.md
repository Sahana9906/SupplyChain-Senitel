# Supply Agreement — Vertex Logistics Group (SUP-049)

**Contract ID:** CTR-049
**Supplier:** Vertex Logistics Group (SUP-049)
**Category:** Textiles
**Region:** Asia, Vietnam
**Effective Date:** 2025-06-25
**Expiry Date:** 2028-06-24

## Commercial Terms

- **Agreed Lead Time:** 20 days from purchase order confirmation to ready-for-shipment.
- **Minimum Order Quantity:** 100 units per purchase order.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 60.
- **Service Level Agreement:** Net 30, 35% on-time delivery target

## Delivery Obligations

The supplier is obligated to notify the buyer's Procurement team in
writing if any purchase order is expected to miss its agreed delivery
window by more than 2 business days. Notification must include a
revised estimated ready date.

## Penalty Terms

5% order value penalty per day late beyond 2-day grace period

## Quality Requirements

All shipments must include a certificate of conformance for the
relevant product category. Lots failing incoming inspection are subject
to the standard Quality Issue Escalation procedure and may be returned
at supplier expense.
