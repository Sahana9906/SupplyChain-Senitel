# Supply Agreement — Keystone Enterprises (SUP-027)

**Contract ID:** CTR-027
**Supplier:** Keystone Enterprises (SUP-027)
**Category:** Hardware
**Region:** Asia, India
**Effective Date:** 2025-07-25
**Expiry Date:** 2026-07-25

## Commercial Terms

- **Agreed Lead Time:** 19 days from purchase order confirmation to ready-for-shipment.
- **Minimum Order Quantity:** 250 units per purchase order.
- **Payment Terms:** 2/10 Net 30.
- **Service Level Agreement:** Net 45, 68% on-time delivery target

## Delivery Obligations

The supplier is obligated to notify the buyer's Procurement team in
writing if any purchase order is expected to miss its agreed delivery
window by more than 2 business days. Notification must include a
revised estimated ready date.

## Penalty Terms

1% order value penalty per day late beyond 5-day grace period

## Quality Requirements

All shipments must include a certificate of conformance for the
relevant product category. Lots failing incoming inspection are subject
to the standard Quality Issue Escalation procedure and may be returned
at supplier expense.
