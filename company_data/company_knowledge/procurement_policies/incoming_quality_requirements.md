# Incoming Quality Requirements

All incoming shipments are subject to inspection against the applicable
product specification prior to release into sellable inventory.

- Lots with a defect rate exceeding 2% by sample count are placed on
  quality hold pending supplier disposition instructions.
- Suppliers must provide a certificate of conformance with each
  shipment for regulated or safety-critical categories.
- Repeated quality holds against the same supplier (2 or more within a
  rolling 90-day period) trigger the standard Quality Issue Escalation
  procedure.
