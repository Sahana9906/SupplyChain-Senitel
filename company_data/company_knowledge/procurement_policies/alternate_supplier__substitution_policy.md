# Alternate Supplier / Substitution Policy

Where a primary supplier for a SKU cannot meet a required delivery date,
Procurement may source the same product from an approved alternate
supplier in the same product category, subject to the following limits:

- Unit cost variance versus the primary supplier must not exceed 15%
  without Category Manager approval, or 25% without Procurement
  Director approval.
- The alternate supplier must hold current Approved Supplier status for
  the relevant category.
- Any substitution must be logged against the original purchase order
  reference for audit purposes.
