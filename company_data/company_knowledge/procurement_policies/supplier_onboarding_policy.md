# Supplier Onboarding Policy

New suppliers must complete the standard qualification process before
receiving a purchase order:

1. Financial and business reference review.
2. Sample or pilot-lot quality evaluation against product specification.
3. Contract negotiation covering lead time, MOQ, SLA, and payment terms.
4. Category Manager and Procurement Director sign-off.

Onboarding typically takes 4-8 weeks depending on product category and
cannot be expedited solely to meet an urgent purchasing need; the
Emergency Purchasing Policy governs urgent needs instead.
