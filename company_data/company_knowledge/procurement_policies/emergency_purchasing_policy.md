# Emergency Purchasing Policy

An emergency purchase order is defined as an order required to prevent a
stockout of a High or Critical criticality SKU within 5 business days.

Emergency POs may bypass standard multi-quote sourcing, subject to
Category Manager approval, and should be flagged as "Emergency" in the
purchasing system. All emergency POs are reviewed retrospectively at the
next monthly procurement meeting to confirm the emergency designation
was warranted.
